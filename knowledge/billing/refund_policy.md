# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers are eligible for refunds when:

- Duplicate charges occurred.
- Incorrect billing is confirmed.
- Service was unavailable for a significant period.
- Billing system errors are verified.

## Processing Time

- Standard refunds: 5 business days
- International refunds: 10 business days

## Refund Methods

Refunds are issued using the original payment method whenever possible.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Refund amount exceeds company limits.
- Payment gateway rejects refund.
- Customer disputes previous refunds.

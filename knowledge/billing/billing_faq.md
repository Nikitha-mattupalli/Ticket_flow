# Billing FAQ

## Why did my bill increase?

Bills may increase because of:

- Higher usage
- Subscription upgrades
- Taxes
- Promotional discounts expiring

---

## How long do refunds take?

Normally within 5 business days.

---

## Can I receive a copy of my invoice?

Yes. Customers can request invoices for completed billing periods.

---

## My payment failed.

Retry the payment or use another payment method.

---

## I was charged twice.

Duplicate transactions will be investigated and refunded if confirmed.

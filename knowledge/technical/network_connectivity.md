# Network connectivity

First confirm whether other websites work. Retry on a second network, disable
VPN or proxy software temporarily, and restart the browser or client. Corporate
networks may need to allow outbound HTTPS traffic on port 443 and the service's
documented domains.

Collect the timestamp, region, client version, and exact error message. Do not
recommend disabling firewalls permanently. Escalate if multiple networks fail
or logs indicate DNS, TLS certificate, or firewall policy errors.
